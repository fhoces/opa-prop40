## How to read this

CRediT roles describe who contributed what, not who is responsible for it. Where the AI
led a role, the human side is recorded as supporting (directing and reviewing); the
matrix does not distinguish a supporting role from no involvement at all.

The **Human verification** row is not part of CRediT. It records how well a human
understands each output, separately from who produced it, on a three-point scale: *No
human review* (no human eyes have reviewed the output), *Lightly checked* (lightly
checked, but cannot be clearly explained) and *Understood* (can be explained to others).
A qualified answer ("understood, except part N") belongs in a footnote, not the cell
(lesson D1). Re-run the questionnaire at each milestone: answers move as understanding
changes, and a record kept as the work happens is more honest than one reconstructed at
the end (hub checklist item 3).
