HOW YOU ARRIVED AT THE SURVEY (entry path: a shared link)

A colleague forwarded you a link with this note, copied from the project author's public post:

"Two analyses of California's Proposition 40 reach opposite conclusions. The proposition would tax Californians worth $1 billion or more once, at 5% of their net worth. Boll, Saez and Zucman estimate that it raises about $104 billion in wealth tax. Jaros and Rauh find that it leaves the state $39 billion poorer, because billionaires who leave stop paying California income tax. On the common definition this project uses, which also counts income tax effects, the first estimate becomes $107 billion. [...] If you work in tax policy, public finance or a related field, I am collecting expert views on the inputs the two analyses disagree on. It takes 5 to 10 minutes. If you pass it on, please share this link rather than the address of the form it opens: fhoces.github.io/opa-prop40/comparison/site/survey"

You clicked the link. The survey opened. You have not visited the project's pages.
