# PAP review, 2026-10-18: internal consistency

This review checks every number, label, question text, section reference and file path in `comparison/survey/PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` against its sources: the power appendix (`comparison/survey/power/power.tex`, `sim.py` and the tables in `power/out/`), the two form copies (`comparison/survey/plan/forms/control.json` and `treatment.json`), the site's survey code (`comparison/R/site.R`, `comparison/survey/draws.gs`, `gas_build.py`, `mock_forms.py`), the registry draft (`comparison/survey/registry-draft.md`) and the two pretest folders (`feedback/008-2026-10-17-rct-pilot/`, `feedback/009-2026-10-17-rct-pilot-2/`). The plan's tables 1 and 4 match `out/summary.tex` and `out/thresholds.tex` entry for entry, the term amounts and shares in section 3 and Appendix A match `dial_path.csv` and the treatment form's page, the question texts in section 4.4 match both forms, and every pretest number quoted in sections 4.4, 5.3 and 10 recomputes from the first pretest's data. The findings below are the mismatches that remain, most important first.

1. **Section 5.3 and 6.1, lines 241 and 266-267. Severity A. AUTHOR DECISION.** The plan defines S3 as three indicators per question adjusted "within each answer type" (three families of five tests), while every other source treats S3 as one family of five tests on the coded answer $y_{ik}$. Plan line 241: "S3. Each of the five $y_{ik}$, reported as three indicators (BSZ value, JR value, neutral answer)." Plan lines 266-267: "The five S3 outcomes are adjusted with the Romano-Wolf step-down procedure within each answer type (section 6.3)." Against: plan line 319, "the five questions of S3" as one family; `power.tex` lines 93-95, "The families are the five question-level tests"; `out/thresholds.tex` line 8, "Each of the five questions & 5 & 150 & 187"; `sim.py` line 284, which simulates the question family on `y` (the -1/0/+1 coding) with five tests; `registry-draft.md` lines 49-50, "S3 each of the five questions separately"; `pilot_analysis.py` lines 140-141, "S3: each question, Romano-Wolf over the five" on `y_q`. The tier-1 threshold of section 7 (150 responses at 0.5 SD) is simulated for five tests on $y_{ik}$, so it does not cover fifteen indicator tests. Fix (source of truth: the appendix and the registry draft, which agree with each other and with the pilot analysis). Replace line 241 with: "S3. Each of the five $y_{ik}$, estimated with the H1 equation. The five tests form one Romano-Wolf family (section 6.3). For each question, the shares of respondents who chose the BSZ value, the JR value and a neutral answer are reported by arm, descriptively." Replace lines 266-267 with: "Secondary outcomes S1 to S3 are estimated with the same equation. S1 and S2 are one test each. The five S3 tests are adjusted together with the Romano-Wolf step-down procedure (section 6.3)." Tagged AUTHOR DECISION because it fixes what S3 tests. It touches the decision of 2026-10-18 to drop per-question multinomial outcomes: the "three indicators" wording reads as a residue of that consideration. If the author wants the three indicators tested instead, the appendix needs a fifteen-test family (or three families of five) and tier 1's threshold changes.
