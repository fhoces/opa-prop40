Read-only review of `feedback/008-2026-10-17-rct-pilot/00-materials/*`, `comparison/survey/PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md`, `comparison/survey/power/power.tex` (plus `out/*.tex` and `macros.tex`), `feedback/005-2026-10-16-explorer-ux/03-landing-survey-plan.md`, `comparison/survey/mock_forms.py`, `gas_build.py`, and the export CSVs the generators read. ## 1. Findings

**F1. Blocking. Q3 compares two things at once and duplicates Q11.** File: `survey-control.txt` lines 26 to 28 (same in treatment, lines 57 to 59); source `mock_forms.py` `split_bundle()`, line 286. Text: "A. 45% of all billionaires' income tax, whoever leaves." / "B. 5%, only the income tax of those who leave." The BSZ value is a rule (the leavers' own tax, in proportion to their wealth). Its "5%" is that rule applied to the BSZ count of leavers (5% of wealth, the answer B of Q11). Under the JR count of leavers (29%) the same rule gives about 29%. So the two choices differ in both the rule and the count of leavers, and a respondent who picks B in Q11 is pushed to B in Q3 for consistency. Two of the five index items then carry one judgment, which inflates the index's weight on departures. The plan's own aim for this split (section 4.4: "so both choices of each answer the same thing") is not met. Replacement: "A. A flat 45% of all billionaires' income tax, whatever share of them leaves: a range of 30% to 60%, set above the share of wealth that has left to cover moves that are not reported." / "B. Only the income tax of those who leave, in proportion to the wealth that leaves: 5% if 5% of the wealth leaves, 29% if 29% does." In the treatment page (line 32) change "only the leavers' own income tax is lost" to "only the leavers' own income tax, in proportion to their wealth".

**F2. Blocking (for registration). The plan has an unresolved author note and open decisions.** File: `PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` line 290 ("SAY SOMETHING ABOUT THE EFFECTS OF BOTS SUBMISSIONS...") and section 11 (six open decisions), section 2 (expected N placeholder), section 9 (close date wording). None can stay in a registered plan. Change: accept or edit the bulk-submission rule at lines 291 to 297, delete line 290's note, fill the expected N, and resolve section 11 before registering.

**F3. Major. The public post promises the survey "names neither analysis"; half of its respondents get a form that names both.** File: `entry-share.txt` line 5. The share link now routes to the experiment, so the promise is false for the treatment arm. It is also the sentence most likely to be quoted onward. Replacement for any new or editable post: "If you work in tax policy, public finance or a related field, I am collecting expert views on the inputs the two analyses disagree on. It takes about 5 minutes: fhoces.github.io/opa-prop40/comparison/site/survey". If the live post cannot be edited, record in the plan (section 10) that respondents by the share path were told the analyses would not be named.

**F4. Major. Q9 is not neutral between its two choices.** File: `survey-control.txt` lines 56 to 57. "A. 100%: the tax is collected in full, as written." gives no reason; "B. 50%: a guesstimated likelihood that the tax will not survive court litigation; it is as likely to fail as to stand." calls the JR value a guess and flips the sign of the question (survives vs will not survive) inside the choice. Replacement: "A. 100%: collected in full as written; the Act is argued to have been drafted to stay clear of constitutional challenges." / "B. 50%: as likely to stand as to fail; the 'more likely than not' threshold an accounting rule uses to judge whether a tax stands."

**F5. Major. Q11's BSZ choice argues against the JR choice and adds a third value.** File: `survey-control.txt` line 68; source `mock_forms.py` `DEPARTED["bsz"]`. "...because press reports do not by themselves establish a change of legal residence. Counting four of them (Page, Thiel, Hankey and Kalanick) is treated as an aggressive scenario." The first clause is a rebuttal of A placed inside B; the second introduces a scenario the respondent is not asked about. B is also twice the length of A. Replacement: "B. 5% of the wealth: an allowance for billionaires who moved in time, with no named billionaire counted, since a press report of a move is not a record of a change of legal residence." Keep A as is.

**F6. Major. There is no denominator for attrition.** File: `PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` section 8, "Attrition". The treatment page sits before the required questions; a treated respondent who quits there leaves no trace, and a binomial test on submitted counts detects only large imbalances (with 100 submissions, roughly 60/40). The 005 plan's item A4/B4 (a `sendBeacon` ping at the draw, no identifying data) gives the denominator for both the attrition check and the response rate. Change: adopt it, fire it on every draw at every entry point (the explorer buttons and the share page's redirect), log `arm` and `entry`, and register "submissions divided by draws, by arm and entry point" as the attrition check, with the binomial test as the fallback.

**F7. Major. H1 cannot see polarization or a pull toward neutral.** File: `PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` sections 5.2 and 6.1. The most plausible effect of naming the studies is that supporters move to BSZ values and opponents to JR values. That leaves the mean of Lean unchanged and H1 at zero. A second plausible effect, more "About equally credible" answers after seeing that one term is 56% of the gap, moves the mean only if the control leans one way. Change: add a secondary outcome S4, "mean |Lean_i|" (how far from neutral), and S5, "the share of respondents whose five answers are all one study's value" (bloc answering), both estimated with the H1 equation. State the polarization mechanism in section 10 and note that support is asked after treatment so it cannot be used to split it.

**F8. Major. The treatment page is too long for a Google Forms page description, and its note paragraph is not needed to answer.** File: `survey-treatment.txt` lines 22 to 50; source `gas_build.py` lines 25 to 29 (`setHelpText`, one block of small grey text, no headings). Line 48 ("The four amounts are the four terms of the equation on the project's comparison page ... splitting it among those three would depend on the order in which they are changed. The other amounts also change somewhat with the order.") is for the plan, not the respondent, who has never seen the equation. Replacement for line 48: "The income tax lost is one amount because its three inputs multiply each other." Also drop "Below, how much of the gap each part explains, and the values each study uses." from line 28, since part 4 gives no values, or add "(part 4 lists no values)".

**F9. Major. The landing panel promises a task the survey does not ask.** File: `entry-landing.txt` lines 9 and 11 (and the 005 plan B3). "record what you think the values of a few key inputs behind the difference are" and "Your answers are compared with the values the two analyses use." The survey asks which of two proposed values is more credible, and treated respondents see the analyses' values at once. Replacement: "Before you see why, we invite you to judge which of two proposed values for each key input is more credible. It takes about five minutes." Move "The survey is primarily for economists and policy analysts." above the buttons so it is read before the choice.

**F10. Major. Forwarded form addresses bypass the draw.** File: `PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` section 4.2 and `comparison/site/survey/index.html`. The share page redirects with `location.replace`, so the address bar shows the Google Forms URL. Anyone who copies that address and forwards it sends every colleague to one arm; the share stratum then contains clusters assigned by one coin. Nothing in the data identifies them. Change: add to section 8 a stated limit, and to the share post and the explorer's Copy link the instruction to share the project link, not the form's address. If the beacon of F6 exists, a form with many submissions and few draws is the signature.

**F11. Major. The 005 plan still describes a different experiment.** File: `03-landing-survey-plan.md`. Leftovers that contradict the plan: the panel A/B by arm with `localStorage` and `?arm=` (B2, lines 87 to 92); groups `explorer-1`, `explorer-2`, `other` and eight forms with the A/B version swap (table at lines 37 to 46, A1, A3); "Reading the result" (lines 140 to 148), which compares panel arms by count; the overview button to the `other` pair (B1) while the plan leaves it open. Change: add a header line: "Superseded in part by comparison/survey/PRE-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md (2026-10-17): the panel is shown to everyone, six forms with one choice order, the draw is control versus treatment at the click. B1's link plumbing, B3's panel and B5's tests stand." Then update B1 to read the `entry,arm,url` layout the plan implies.

**F12. Major. Only the JR study carries an affiliation on the treatment page.** File: `survey-treatment.txt` lines 23 to 24; source `mock_forms.py` `STUDIES`. "Boll, Saez and Zucman (BSZ, August 2026)" against "Jaros and Rauh (JR, NBER, September 2026)". In an experiment about naming, one prestige cue on one side is a confound in the treatment's content. Replacement: drop "NBER" here, or give both venues ("a UC Berkeley working paper" / "an NBER chapter"). The same asymmetry is on the explorer; leave it there, the survey copy is what matters.

**F13. Minor. Choice order is not balanced.** The JR value is listed first in four of the five required questions (Q3, Q5, Q7, Q11) and the BSZ value first in all five optional ones. The order is the same in both arms, so it does not bias H1, but a primacy effect leans the control index toward JR and makes S3 differ across questions for a reason unrelated to content. Change: in `gas_build.py`, swap the order in Q5 and Q7 at build time (three JR-first, two BSZ-first), record the order in the Key tab.

**F14. Minor. The description promises a reason for every value; several have none.** File: `survey-control.txt` line 3 ("the reason given for each") against Q3 (both), Q9 A, Q14 B ("None."), Q16 B ("Left out of the base."), Q17, Q18 B ("Not counted."). Change: "two values that have been proposed for it, with the reason given where there is one", or give each a short reason (F1 and F4 cover Q3 and Q9).

**F15. Minor. "These are empirical quantities" overclaims.** File: `survey-control.txt` line 5. Replacement: "Most of these are empirical quantities, and none should depend on whether one supports or opposes a wealth tax."

**F16. Minor. Q21 "Verify your identity with your email."** Nothing is verified. Replacement: title "Your email (optional)", help "If you give one, your answers are also reported in a separate analysis of named respondents."

**F17. Minor. Q13's gate tells respondents the next page does not matter.** "only a small, second-order effect". Fine for a descriptive page, but "second-order" is jargon. Replacement: "The last five inputs move the estimate only a little. Would you like to assess them anyway?"

**F19. Minor. Q5 B "treating the responses as temporary".** "Responses" is unglossed. Replacement: "treating the departures as temporary."

**F20. Minor. The share post's "$104 billion in wealth tax" and the treatment page's "+$106.8 billion" are different numbers for the same study.** A treated share-path respondent meets both within a minute. ## 2. Questions only the author can answer

1. Expected number of responses (plan section 2). At 50 the main test detects only 0.79 SD; is a pilot of that size still worth registering, or is the registration mainly for the later full run?
2. Proposition 40 is on the 3 November 2026 ballot. The close is 4 November. Should responses after the vote count, and should the vote date be a recorded covariate?
3. Should support for Proposition 40 be asked before treatment, so polarization (F7) can be tested by subgroup? The cost is priming both arms.
4. Were the six copies made with "collect email" and "limit to one response" off (the 005 plan's A2)? Either setting changes who can answer.
5. Can the public post in `entry-share.txt` still be edited (F3, F20), and has the share link already been emailed with that text?
6. The overview's survey button: the `explorer` pair or a fourth pair (plan section 11, item 1)?
7. Is the bundled treatment the intended estimand (the comparison page as the site shows it), or would a labels-only third arm be run later if N allows? The power appendix should say a third arm is out of scope at expected N.
8. Has the opt-in branch (Q13's "No, skip them") been tested on a treatment form after the page break is inserted at index 2 (`gas_build.py` line 141)? The copies keep the goto page object, so it should hold, but only a submitted test shows it.

## 3. The three changes to make first

1. Rewrite Q3 so it compares the two rules and not the two counts of leavers (F1), and check the index's five items are five distinct judgments.
2. Make Q9 and Q11 neutral (F4, F5): no reason on one side only, no rebuttal inside a choice, no "guesstimated".
3. Record every draw (F6) before launch, so attrition and response rates have a denominator; then resolve the plan's open notes (F2) and register.
