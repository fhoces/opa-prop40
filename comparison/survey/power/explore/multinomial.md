# Per-question multinomial outcomes: power (exploration, not registered)

Model calibration repeated from the power appendix: tau = 0.646 (index SD 0.5); the latent shift for 0.5 SD on the lean index is 0.432, for 0.3 SD 0.248.

The lean index (H1) needs **126** responses for 0.5 SD and **349** for 0.3 SD (analytic; the appendix's simulated 349 for 0.3 SD matches the formula's 349).
