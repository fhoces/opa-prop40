## How to read this

CRediT roles describe who contributed what, not who is responsible for it.

The **Human verification** row is not part of CRediT. It records how well a human understands each output, separately from who produced it: *No human review* (no human eyes have reviewed the output), *Lightly checked* (checked, but cannot yet be clearly explained) and *Understood* (can be explained to others). The overview page's table shows these three levels as *not reviewed*, *lightly reviewed* and *reviewed*.
